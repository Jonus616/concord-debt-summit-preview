Event Details

Concord Action is hosting the National Citizens Debt Summit in Washington, D.C. March 2-4, 2027. This is a unique opportunity to join other Concord advocates from around the country to send a message to Congress: we must cut the debt and save America's future.

1775

Ordinary people, mostly farmers called Minutemen, answered a call to arms. They courageously stood together in the town of Concord, Massachusetts, against thousands of British soldiers, and successfully turned them back to Boston.

$40T

This summit is a launching point for a national movement focused on restoring fiscal responsibility and strengthening America's long-term economic future. With the national debt approaching $40 trillion, Americans are already feeling the effects through rising inflation and high interest rates. Without action to tackle the almost $2 trillion annual deficit, these problems will worsen rapidly.

2027

The 2027 National Citizens Debt Summit will bring together advocates, leaders, and changemakers from across the country around this issue. By taking part in the summit, you will become a Concord Action policy advocate influencing legislators directly and learning from national experts how to grow this movement in your own community.

Summit participants will

  1. 01Be a part of exclusive briefings from the nation's top experts and policymakers on the federal debt and deficit
  2. 02Network with Concord Action advocates from around the country who are advancing the mission in their communities to learn and grow this movement together
  3. 03Meet with your member of Congress and key Washington decision-makers to make the case directly to national leaders to get control of the federal deficit NOW
  4. 04Be a direct part of Concord Action's mission to restore fiscal sanity in Washington D.C. and build a brighter future for generations of Americans to come
